## Owned-photo replacement checklist

- Record the photographer, shoot date, and asset owner.
- Record adult model consent or guardian consent for minors, permitted channels, term, and withdrawal contact.
- Confirm that no private information, third-party artwork, or unapproved branding appears.
- Keep desktop and mobile crops with hands, instruments, sheet music, faces, and entrance identifiers intact.
- Replace the stock caption and set `source: 'owned'` only after the records above are complete.

The legacy files under `public/images/blog/` have no supplied provenance record. They are retained in the repository for compatibility but are not displayed by the redesigned blog until their rights are verified.
